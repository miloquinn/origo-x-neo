Origo X uses separate non-consumable products: app lifetime unlock (com.niki.xxread.reader.lifetime, US $9.99), a zero-price 14-day reading trial (com.niki.xxread.reader.trial14d), and account Premium lifetime (com.niki.xxread.premium.lifetime.v2, US $8.99). StoreKit displays localized prices. No product auto-renews.

Use App unlock in the account center for guest reading purchase, trial or restore. No Origo sign-in is required. Trial is limited to local reading and expires 14 days after the original transaction.

Premium becomes visible only after permanent app unlock, then requires Origo sign-in. It enables more user-imported source formats and trusted private-network sources. It supplies no commercial books or source catalog and does not include app reading rights. Store apps have no external card-code redemption entry.

Reader restoration uses the original Apple Account. Premium restoration additionally requires its bound Origo account. Deleting an Origo account terminates account Premium without transferring it; independent reader ownership remains restorable. Legacy purchases retain their original promised bundle through the legacy restore path.

Sandbox purchases are verified server-side and enable the test session only; restore after restarting. Test purchase, restore, cancellation and refund on a real test device before submission. Complete metadata does not mean review approval.
