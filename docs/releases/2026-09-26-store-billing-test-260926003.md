## Expected behavior

1. Store builds offer a 14-day reader trial and a separate permanent app unlock (US$9.99 base price). Buying/restoring app access does not require an Origo account.
2. Premium is hidden until permanent app ownership is confirmed. Purchasing Premium requires an Origo account and uses the platform store (US$8.99 base price).
3. Premium expands supported source formats; it does not replace the app unlock. Restoring historical bundled products preserves their original entitlement.
4. Website builds include permanent reader access and retain account card redemption for Premium. Store builds do not offer private redemption codes.
5. Account deletion terminates account Premium while independently owned store app access remains restorable.

- Signed out: start trial / restore app ownership; verify Premium is hidden while on trial.

- Sign in to an Origo test account, then buy Premium; verify additional source formats unlock for that account.
- Cancel and decline purchases; verify no entitlement is granted.
- Restore purchases and switch Origo accounts; reader ownership stays independent, Premium does not transfer between accounts.
- Verify historical purchase restoration and refund revocation separately.
