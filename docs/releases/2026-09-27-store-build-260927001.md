## Policy evidence

Apple 3.1.1 explicitly restricts custom license-key unlocking. Removing a shop
link does not remove that restriction. Platform-generated offer codes use the
supported StoreKit flow. Google official promo codes follow Play billing; an
external paid-code path must not be presented as assured policy compliance.

- https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-in-app-purchases/create-offer-codes-for-in-app-purchases
- https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/promo
- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en
